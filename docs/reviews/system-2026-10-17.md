## Verdict

**CHANGES REQUIRED ACROSS DESIGNS: SYS-1 to SYS-6 block later work; none reopens an owner
goal.** The designs agree with PLAN's goals and, in direction, with each other. Four interfaces
are defined differently by two or more designs (SYS-1, SYS-2, SYS-3, SYS-9), four
prerequisites have no owner (SYS-4), and two designs depend on breaking one PLAN rule (SYS-6).

What holds, and reproduces:

- The four strata branches merge pairwise without textual conflict, except format 13 with the
  layers refactor (`merge_probe.out`). The simulator and automata touch only new files and
  three or four lines of `CMakeLists.txt` each.
- Track C's scale numbers hold together, and the simulator adopts them: 64 distinct peers per
  node give 256 keepalive frames per second over 128 connections at rest (TC:664-667;
  SIM:45), and a quiescent leader's node failing elects within 4 s (TC:840-841; SIM:83).
- The simulator's population adds up: five index tiers make 9,968,800 shards, 300 voter
  replicas per node, and a mean shard of 3.08 GiB (SIM:40-43).
- Pins agree across consumers: strata and `journal` both pin `completion` `5251cf9`, which
  carries the macOS per-device flush (`86ca1a2`).
- The library additions transport security needed are in and opt-in: `reactor` `e19afd9`
  (`strict`) and `http` `f93aef7` (idle, header, and inactivity timeouts, the failure hook).
- `consensus`'s dialer already binds the node it meant to reach (`peers.h:344`), TS-R2's first
  requirement, and refuses a leaf with no pin (`tls.h:48-64`).
- Every new design routes its owner questions instead of deciding them: simulator Q1 to Q6
  (SIM:409-434), automata O1 to O5 (AUT:323-336), PKI Q1 to Q6 (PKI:288-348). The failover
  contributor routed its durability trade, and the index carries it (README.md:13).
- Format 13 consolidates rather than reopens (F13:3-16) and names its own open coordination
  with the layers branch (F13:489-493).

What blocks:

- SYS-1: format 13 and the layers refactor build the same format-13 items on two code
  structures; 30 conflict hunks today, and four semantic conflicts.
- SYS-2: one peer transport is assumed by five designs, with three answers on peer identity
  and no home for the node directory.
- SYS-3: the wire refuses mixed versions, the simulator assumes them, and Detent may freeze
  DTC2 before Track C finishes it.
- SYS-4: format 13's freeze, Phase 3R's rows, and C2's budgets wait on work nobody holds.
- SYS-5: main failed CI on three platforms for six hours; the gate run measures that state.
- SYS-6: two designs need branch measurements that PLAN forbids.

     `index_sort` is a plugin in L2's layouts.
  3. *File order.* F5's tail is `docids`, `stats`, `lengths`, `norms`, `versions`,

     (inverted) after `versions` (keys) is not, and `lengths` is "first use", not pinned

### SYS-2. One peer transport, five designs, three answers on peer identity

Designs: Track C (D1, D5, D12, D16); simulator (D4, D7, Q3); PKI (D5, Q3, Q5, Q6) and its
security review; transport security (deferred D2 to D8, answer 2); DISTRIBUTION (Layers,
open decision 2); PLAN's Track D2.

Evidence:

- **Identity.** `consensus` admits a peer by the SHA-256 of its leaf's DER, one configured pin
  per node (`tls.h:45-66`), and Track C keeps pins, moved into a node directory (TC:212-215,
  766-771). Transport security answers "Leaf pins: no" and binds cluster and node name in the
  SAN (TS:153-154; TS-R2). PKI defines a URI SAN and leaves the choice to strata, "decided
  there" (PKI:312-324). PKI's security review recommends SAN identity for Xapiand and an SPKI
  pin for Detent (PKISEC:37-43, 79-90). Xapiand hosts strata's groups on `consensus`'s
  transport from Phase 7b (PLAN.md:537-540; ARCHITECTURE.md:340), so those answers put two
  identity mechanisms into one transport library.
- **The node directory has no home.** Track C puts it in "the root group's node table under
  Track D" (TC:213-214). DISTRIBUTION's root holds the "placement-group map only"
  (DISTRIBUTION.md:44). The simulator's contract and tier A model no directory (SIM:36-92,
  234-237).
- **Its cost depends on the identity choice.** A DER pin changes at every renewal
  (PKISEC:79-83). At 100,000 nodes on 365-day leaves that is 274 directory changes a day, and
  1,111 a day on Q5's 90-day option (PKI:335-340). An SPKI pin with same-key renewal
  (PKI:208-209), or URI identity, makes none. Offline issuance (PKI D2, D6) also turns the
  simulator's 11 node replacements a day at nominal churn (SIM:58), and every renewal, into a
  manual request carried to the authority host.
- **Discovery is one decision with two homes and no owner.** Transport security's deferred D3
  (authenticated discovery, carrying TS-R3's and TS-R4's replay and nonce rules, TS:126) and
  DISTRIBUTION's open decision 2 (SWIM over a bounded connection cache, or a datagram path
  beside the transport, DISTRIBUTION.md:129-135). The simulator models datagrams "if D2
  chooses" (SIM:143-144).
- **Enrollment is deferred twice** to a transport with no enrollment role: PKI Q6
  (PKI:342-348) and transport security's D5 (TS:128).
- **The simulator's Q3** needs `Peers`' send policy as a sans-I/O class "done with C4's D16"
  (SIM:418-422). D16's text does not include it (TC:749-773).
- **PKISEC SR1's fix** sets `X509_CHECK_FLAG_NEVER_CHECK_SUBJECT` in
  `reactor::tls::bind_hostname` (PKISEC:122-125; `reactor_tls.h:91-102`). Detent calls it in
  its delivery client (`src/detent_delivery/connection_cache.cc:81, 112`) and its HTTPS client
  (`include/detent_transport/https_client.hh:17`). It is a behavior change for Detent,
  additive only as an opt-in.

Who acts:

- The owner decides the identity model once, for `consensus`'s transport: DER pin, SPKI pin,
  or a cluster CA with the URI SAN and the role in the path (PKISEC SR2).
- The lead assigns one design for the peer transport: C4's D16, extended to identity, the
  node directory and its home, the discovery transport, enrollment, and the sans-I/O send
  policy, with TS-R2 to TS-R8, TS-R13, and PKI's profile as requirements. DISTRIBUTION's root
  row changes to match.
- The security reviewer reviews that design's threat model. Detent's maintainer agrees to the
  `consensus::transport` changes, since pins are Detent's authority model (PKI:46).
- The PKI contributor states revision 1's behavior at 100,000 nodes: issuance and renewals per
  day, a planned rotation as three rolling restarts of every node (PKI:211-222), and an
  emergency rotation as one restart of every node at once (PKI:224-227).

### SYS-3. The wire refuses mixed versions; the simulator assumes them; Detent may freeze DTC2 first

Designs: Track C (D2, the coordinated commit); simulator (D1's churn table); PLAN's Track D8;
Detent's adoption blueprint (Group F) and Pass 3; layers (L8, L16: digests and installs
across binaries).

Evidence:

- `validate_hello` refuses any profile difference (`hello.h:48-52`). DTPROF02 will cover the
  wire version (TC:138-140), DTC2's decoder rejects unknown tags and "a tag added later bumps
  the version" (TC:177-180), and "two wire paths ... are not kept" (TC:136-137). Two binaries
  on two wire versions cannot connect.
- The simulator upgrades the whole cluster in 24 h, one rack per zone at a time, and
  "nothing re-replicates" (SIM:65). Under the hello rule each upgraded node loses its
  cross-version peers, the node-pair keepalive declares them down after three missed 500 ms
  pings (TC:647-650), and placement starts replacements after its hold. No design defines
  mixed-version operation: Track D8 (PLAN.md:444) has none.
- Track C fixes the rest of DTC2 before its coordinated commit: the frame header with group id
  and incarnations, C2's coalesced heartbeats, `Wake`, `LeaderGone`, `Ping`, and `Pong`, and
  C4's window updates and chunk frames (TC:49-50, 194-199; LIBRARY_BOUNDARIES.md:313). The
  codec at `fc66c0e` has tags 1 to 8 and no group header (`codec.h:22-40`). Detent's
  blueprint adopts "latest consensus" now, with a coordinated restart, and "future
  incompatible frames require a new version or a designed transition"
  (LIBRARY_ADOPTION_ARCHITECTURE.md:79, 87). If Detent ships today's DTC2, C2's and C4's
  frames become a DTC3, and `consensus` either carries two wire versions or Detent takes a
  second flag day.
- At 100,000 nodes a flag day is a cluster-wide outage, so mixed versions are part of the
  contract (PLAN.md:36-41; DISTRIBUTION.md:12-22).

Who acts:

- The lead and Detent's maintainer agree on the order before Group F adopts: Detent waits for
  the full DTC2 frame set, or Track C names the later frames DTC3 and designs N − 1
  acceptance.
- The owner rules on D8's scope: rolling upgrades across wire versions at the contract.
- Track C's next revision replaces the hello's profile equality with a version range (N and
  N − 1) before the remaining tags are fixed.
- The simulator contributor models version skew as refused connections until D8 exists, and
  its rolling-upgrade row cites D8.
